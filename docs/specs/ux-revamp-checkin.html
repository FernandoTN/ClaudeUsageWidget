The model: two words, three pointers

freeViewing

The one account the popover, the inspector and single-account bar mode show and operate on. Clicking a tile, picking a name in the popover, choosing a row in Accounts, the ⌘-hotkey — all of these only view. Never touches a CLI, never records a switch, never needs the dead-login gate. (Storage: today's activeProfileId, unchanged.)

costly · deliberateActive for Claude · Active for Codex · Active for Grok

The three accounts the CLIs actually use. Changed only by "Make active for <provider>…" (always with a confirmation that states the cost and the candidate's evidence), by the auto-switch, or by a login you made on the CLI side that the sweep adopts (reported as "Active for Claude changed outside the app: now dLeo"). The cyan tile label keeps meaning exactly this. (Storage: the two existing pointers + a new Grok one.)

Rule everywhere: when the account you are viewing is not the provider's active one, both facts are shown side by side — Viewing dJormun · Active for Claude: dRir. The word "Active" without a provider is never displayed again. Every number carries provenance + age ("own · 28 s ago", "headers · 3 m ago"), so a blind account reads as blind, not as a stale number that looks live.

S2b · a setting

Three selector items, one per provider (Cl / Cx / Gk, ~78 pt)

The literal reading of your ask. Same menu builder, filtered to one provider per item. Costs 54 pt more than S2a and repeats what the fleet tiles already show (the active account is on them); loses the one-click overview of all three owners. Kept as a Display setting ("Selector: one item / one per provider") because it is nearly free to offer.

S1 · later, after measuring

Right-click (or ⌥-click) on each provider tile opens that provider's selector

Zero bar width; left-click = view, right-click = select. But composite status items on macOS 27 deliver synthesized click events (the "always opens Commits" incident) and right-click delivery through the group button has not been measured; a selector that sometimes opens the popover instead is worse than none. Planned as an accelerator in a later stage once measured, not as the primary surface.

S3 · mirror only

No new item: the selector as a card inside the dashboard and a Settings page

Zero bar cost, but it keeps the switching control inside the viewing surface — the conflation you asked to end — and a popover closes on the outside click a confirmation needs. The card is still built (Settings › Active & Auto-switch; the dashboard's section header links to the ⇄ menu), as a mirror, never as the only selector.

Decision 2 — selector on by default, and the confirmation

RECOMMENDED

On by default · confirm every manual switch, suppressible per provider

It is what you asked for and it is additive (one toggle in Display removes it). The confirmation carries the cost sentence and the candidate's evidence; "Don't ask again for Claude" is a per-provider checkbox, resettable in Advanced. Auto-switches never confirm (they already meet the thresholds and the queue you set).

Alternatives

Off by default — you enable it once. Always confirm — no suppression checkbox. Never confirm — the menu row's text carries the cost, one click switches (fastest, least deliberate).

Decision 4 — the one habit change: the popover's name menu and the ⌘-hotkey become view-only

Today picking a name in the popover menu (and the "next profile" hotkey) switches the CLI. Under the model above they only view; switching lives in the ⇄ menu, the inspector header, and the dashboard's "Make active…" (which already confirms in two steps). The hotkey also stops crossing providers (today it can hand a Claude session a Codex login — audit M7). This is the change you will feel in your fingers on day one.

Dashboard depth (stage 4) — pick the order

B2 already shows: active card with gauges + thresholds + provenance + suspected caveat + ETA, next candidate with verdict age, the queue slice, two-line roster rows with chips and repair links, account detail, "Make active…" two-step, five recent switches. Stage 4 adds, all additive:

  1. Reset timeline — the next 7 days with a marker per account at its weekly / Fable reset and the headroom that returns (when does capacity come back).
  2. Blind spots — per active account: seconds since the last own measurement, provenance of the shown number, header-rescue count per hour, backoff state (tonight's 3-minute blind window, 19:35–19:38).
  3. Burn rate — pp/min with the last samples as a sparkline; why an account is projected to cross, not just when.
  4. Switch log — the full 30-entry ring with provider filter, trigger and reason.
  5. Rate-limit incidents — transcript tripwire events, stamps, probe 429s, per account, last 24 h.
  6. Roster filters — readiness, provenance, stale > N min, queued only.
  7. Capacity remaining — Σ (100 − weekly) over live accounts, per provider.
