Everything else is merged and deployed (main 6a5eab2); the final frame-by-frame pass came back clean. These three were deliberately left for you. Pick one card per question; the recommendation is marked. Free text at the bottom of each.

1 · The fleet block on the bar (opt-in layouts only)

The reviewer proposed a single-line composition instead of the two-row block. The two-row block is what the three-way consult settled and what the pixel passes polished; changing it is a design decision, not a fix. Frames below are the real renders (4× zoom).

Recommended

A · Keep the two-row block (as shipped)

One dot per account (ready / near limit / exhausted / dead), the active account's digits, the arrow to the next candidate with the login verdict. Every account is visible at a glance; 25 accounts still fit.

Cost: ~89 pt per provider on the bar (vs ~600 pt today for every-account).

B · Single line: "Cl 78 % → dJo ✓" + a status strip

Cl 78 % → dJo ✓

C · One dot row + three numerals

This is the existing "Active + counts" layout: ●3 ▲7 ×1 above the candidate row. Already selectable in Settings today; choosing C means making it the recommended fleet layout instead of dots.

2 · The detached dashboard window

The reviewer asked for two columns when the dashboard is dragged off the bar into its own window. The length problem that motivated it is already solved: rosters collapse to three rows with "Show all N", and the section headers stay pinned while scrolling.

Recommended

A · One column, 380 pt, same in the popover and the window

One layout to keep pixel-clean; the window is the rarer surface. Nothing changes.

Cost: none.

B · Two columns when detached

Providers side by side (Claude left, Codex + Grok right) at ~760 pt, Insights spanning both. Better for a 24-account roster left open on a second display.

Cost: a second layout to keep pixel-clean and re-render in every design pass (~½ day, then ongoing).

C · Resizable detached window, sections flow

The window resizes; sections reflow into as many columns as fit. Most flexible, least predictable in the frame passes.

Cost: ~1 day, plus harness frames at two widths.
